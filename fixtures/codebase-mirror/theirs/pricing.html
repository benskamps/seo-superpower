Billfold pricing

One plan at fourteen dollars a month. Annual billing costs the same as ten months. There is no per-invoice fee, no seat charge under five people, and no contract — the cancel button is in settings and works immediately.

Is there a per-invoice fee?

No. Send four invoices a month or four hundred; the price is the same. Your payment processor still takes its own cut, which Billfold shows on each invoice so the number in your books matches the number in your bank.

What happens when you cancel?

Your data stays exportable for ninety days and the CSV export includes every invoice, client, and payment record. Nothing is held hostage behind a re-subscribe.

Do you offer a freelancer discount?

The single plan is already the freelancer plan. Students and registered nonprofits get half off on request, verified once a year.

How does billing work across currencies?

You are billed in your own currency at the rate on renewal day, and your clients are billed in theirs. See the first invoice guide for how the conversion appears on the document itself.
