Invoicing built for freelancers

Billfold turns the hours you already tracked into an invoice, sends it, and follows up until the money lands. It is the paperwork half of freelancing, automated, so the working half is the only part you think about.

How does Billfold shorten payment times?

Invoices go out the moment a project closes rather than at the end of the month, and the reminder schedule starts on day one instead of day thirty. Two changes to timing move the median payment from thirty-four days to nineteen.

What does an invoice include?

Line items pulled from tracked time, your tax details, the client purchase order number, payment links for card and bank transfer, and a plain-language late fee clause that holds up in most jurisdictions.

Can I bill in more than one currency?

Yes. Each client carries its own currency and tax profile, and totals convert at the rate on the day of issue, which is the rate your accountant expects.

Who is Billfold not for?

Agencies over twenty seats, anyone needing inventory, and teams who bill on milestone schedules deeper than three stages. Those need a real ERP.

Getting started with your first invoice

Import your client list from a CSV, connect a payment processor, and send. The first invoice walkthrough covers the tax fields, and the late payment guide covers the reminder cadence. Both take under ten minutes to work through.
